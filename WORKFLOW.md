# LocalLift AI — Product Workflow

## Owner workflow

```text
1. Open LocalLift AI
2. Create or choose business profile
3. Enter offer, objective, audience, and tone
4. Click Generate campaign
5. Review strategy and content cards
6. Edit or regenerate individual assets
7. Confirm prices, dates, and contact details
8. Approve campaign
9. Copy assets or download the campaign package
10. Return to dashboard for the next campaign
```

## Screen-by-screen requirements

### Landing

Headline: “Your next local campaign, ready in 60 seconds.”

Primary CTA: “Create my campaign”

Secondary CTA: “See how it works”

### Onboarding

Collect only the minimum information needed to produce useful content. Use progressive disclosure for optional logo and brand settings.

### Campaign brief

Use a single focused form with an example offer. Show an estimated result: “You’ll get 3 posts, 1 message, 3 headlines, and a 7-day plan.”

### Campaign result

Put the most useful asset first. Use tabs or sections for Social, Message, Headlines, Schedule, and Visual. Keep copy and regenerate actions close to each asset.

### Approval

The final step should summarize what is ready and what still needs confirmation. Approval changes the status to Approved; it does not publish externally.

### Dashboard

Show campaign cards with objective, status, date, and next action. Avoid complex analytics in the MVP.

## AI-assisted build workflow

1. Ask Antigravity to scaffold the route and component structure.
2. Give it the PRD and design system as constraints.
3. Implement one vertical slice before adding polish.
4. Ask for small, testable changes rather than a full application in one prompt.
5. After each change, run the app and verify the main flow visually.
6. Keep a working checkpoint after profile, generation, and approval milestones.

LocalLift turns those facts into a complete campaign: captions, a WhatsApp message, headlines, a schedule, and a visual. The owner remains in control, edits the copy, confirms the business details, and approves the campaign. That is the gap we are closing for local businesses.”
